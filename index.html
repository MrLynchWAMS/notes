How to Add Notes to Your Science Journal

1

Step 1: Copy

Click the big blue "Copy Notes to Clipboard" button above.

2

Step 2: Open Journal

Open your Dynalist Science Journal tab and click on an empty bullet line where you want the notes to start.

3

Step 3: Paste

Press Ctrl + V to paste. All tabs, indentation, and color tags will format automatically!
